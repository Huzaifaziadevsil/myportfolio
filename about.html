About Me

My professional journey, mission, and vision in Artificial Intelligence.

In the fast-evolving era of Artificial Intelligence, I found my passion in solving complex business challenges through data-driven engineering. My journey began with rigorous self-study and online coursework, which rapidly accelerated into real-world impact.

My professional career started as an intern at Devsinc, where I built a solid foundation in software engineering and data practices. I also expanded my horizons by working as a specialized freelancer on Upwork, delivering custom machine learning models and automated data pipelines to international clients. This dual experience taught me not only how to code, but how to communicate, manage expectations, and deliver ROI-focused solutions.

Today, I am proud to serve as a Full Stack AI Engineer at IMTA. In this role, I don't just train models in isolation—I design and deploy end-to-end AI architectures. My day-to-day involves fine-tuning Large Language Models (LLMs), building complex Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, deploying scalable Computer Vision systems, and automating critical workflows using cutting-edge AI Agents.

My Mission: To bridge the gap between raw data and actionable intelligence by building robust, overfitting-free, production-ready AI solutions that drastically reduce manual effort and generate measurable business value.

My Vision: To be at the forefront of the AI revolution, architecting intelligent systems that empower humans to focus on creativity and strategy, while machines handle computation and automation.
